I’m Yash Prajapati, a Mobile Application Developer with professional experience building and delivering cross-platform applications at production scale. I specialize in Flutter-based development and focus on writing clean, maintainable, and well-architected code that performs reliably in real-world environments.


With a strong foundation in Electronics and Communication Engineering, I approach software with structured thinking and system-level clarity. I am comfortable taking ownership of the full development lifecycle — from architectural decisions and implementation to optimization, testing, and deployment.


My work reflects attention to performance, scalability, security, and long-term maintainability. I am proficient in Dart, Java, C++, and modern development tools, and I actively strengthen my problem-solving ability through Data Structures and Algorithms. Alongside mobile development, I continue expanding my backend knowledge with Node.js and MongoDB to build more scalable and complete systems.


I am enthusiastic about continuous learning, engineering depth, and building products that are technically sound and thoughtfully designed. My goal is to grow as a well-rounded software engineer who delivers reliable solutions and contributes meaningfully to innovative teams.
